# IDD-0005.spec-learning-progress-and-gamification

## Intent

Trainer applications motivate sustained learning through visible daily goals,
mastery progress, streaks, and celebrations while scheduling practice according
to each learner's needs.

## Behavior

- Progress records correct, incorrect, and skipped attempts in time-based
  clusters for each card.
- A card becomes learned only after the required amount of practice and the
  required recent accuracy for its difficulty or content family.
- Normal scheduling excludes learned cards and selects eligible unlearned cards
  with priority for weakness, new material, and recent mistakes while reducing
  immediate repeats.
- Daily attempt and new-card limits shape the daily study plan.
- Sessions show a target, persist completed-card and duration statistics, and
  contribute to the learner's daily activity.
- The streak represents consecutive days with completed sessions.
- When a learner masters a card, the app shows a celebration before continuing
  the session.

## Durable Architecture And Constraints

Progress and gamification semantics are shared by all trainer applications;
content may provide family-specific difficulty or mastery requirements.

## Non-Goals

- A points economy, purchases, leaderboards, or social competition.
- Returning learned cards to normal scheduling without an explicit product
  change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Repeated answers affect progress and mastery according to the card's learning
  requirements.
- Learned cards are omitted from normal selection.
- Daily plans, session statistics, and streaks reflect completed study activity.
- Mastering a card produces one clear celebration event without changing the
  learner's ability to continue the session.

## Verification

- Automated coverage establishes mastery, scheduling, cooldown, and daily-limit
  behavior.
- Automated coverage establishes persistence of session statistics and streaks.
- Automated UI coverage establishes celebration and continuation behavior.
