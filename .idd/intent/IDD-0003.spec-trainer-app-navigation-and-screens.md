# IDD-0003.spec-trainer-app-navigation-and-screens

## Intent

Every trainer application provides a predictable flow between planning a study
session, completing exercises, inspecting learning results, configuring the
experience, and reading app information.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001
- IDD-0002
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005

## Behavior

- The home screen shows the learner's current daily study plan, progress toward
  it, current streak, and a way to begin training when cards remain.
- The training screen presents one exercise at a time, its applicable controls,
  feedback, and session completion information.
- The statistics screen shows overall and per-content learning progress,
  daily activity, session information, and the current streak.
- The settings screen lets the learner control supported learning preferences,
  including the language context where the application supports it.
- The about screen provides app-specific information and external links.
- Debug controls are development-only and are not a product screen or a
  user-facing capability.

## Durable Architecture And Constraints

Applications may vary in branding, content presentation, and app-specific
information while preserving the shared screen responsibilities.

## Non-Goals

- A requirement for identical visual design across trainer applications.
- Exposing development-only controls in production use.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A learner can start a session from the daily plan and return to the home
  screen after ending it.
- Statistics, settings, and about information are reachable outside an active
  exercise.
- The training screen communicates exercise feedback and session completion.

## Verification

- Automated UI coverage establishes access to the shared screen areas.
- Automated UI coverage establishes that training lifecycle transitions return
  the learner to a usable application state.
