# IDD-0006.spec-shared-trainer-app-integration

## Intent

The product family supports multiple focused language-trainer applications that
share one consistent learning experience while each application owns its
identity and subject matter.

## Behavior

- Each trainer application supplies its own name, branding, app information,
  content catalog, and content-specific presentation where needed.
- Each content catalog supplies exercise families, cards, language material, and
  the interaction modes appropriate to that content.
- The shared trainer experience provides session lifecycle, scheduling, progress,
  settings, statistics, and common training screens.
- A new trainer application joins the product family by supplying its own app
  and content while participating in the shared trainer experience.

## Durable Architecture And Constraints

The dependency direction is application to content and shared trainer platform;
content may depend on the shared trainer platform; the shared trainer platform
does not depend on any individual application's branding or content.

## Non-Goals

- Moving app-specific identity or content metadata into the shared platform.
- Making every trainer application use identical content or branding.

## Acceptance Criteria

- NumberGym and VerbGym can use shared learning behavior without sharing their
  branding or subject-specific content.
- A future trainer can be added without requiring the shared platform to depend
  on that trainer's content or identity.

## Verification

- Automated integration coverage establishes that each application can provide
  its catalog to the shared trainer experience.
- Architecture checks establish the intended dependency direction.
