# IDD-0001.spec-numbergym-number-language-training

## Intent

NumberGym helps learners build fast, confident spoken and recognition skills
for numbers through short training sessions.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0003
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005
- IDD-0006
- IDD-0007

## Behavior

- NumberGym provides exercises for digits, larger numbers, times, and phone
  number formats.
- It supports English, Spanish, French, German, and Hebrew. Progress and
  settings are scoped to the selected learning language.
- Number, time, and phone content exposes only the training modes that are
  appropriate for that family.
- Speech exercises require speech recognition; listening exercises require
  text-to-speech for the selected language. An unavailable required capability
  pauses the affected exercise with an explanation.
- Training has no cloud pronunciation analysis and does not require a network
  connection for multiple-choice exercises. Unavailable device services are
  excluded from automatic scheduling rather than blocking compatible drills.
- Device-provided speech recognition and text-to-speech may require installed
  offline language packs; their operation without internet is platform-dependent.
- NumberGym keeps settings and learning progress on the device. It does not
  provide cloud synchronization or multi-device conflict resolution.

## Durable Architecture And Constraints

NumberGym supplies its own content and branded application experience while
using the shared trainer platform described by IDD-0006 and IDD-0007.

## Non-Goals

- Automatic review of learned cards in the normal training flow.
- Cloud synchronization or shared multi-device progress.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A learner can train number, time, and phone content through compatible
  interaction modes.
- Changing the learning language keeps settings and progress distinct for each
  language.
- A required unavailable device capability never produces an unusable exercise.
- Without internet and speech services, automatic scheduling still offers
  compatible multiple-choice exercises for the available content families.

## Verification

- Automated coverage establishes content-family and mode compatibility.
- Automated coverage establishes language-scoped progress and settings.
- Automated coverage establishes that unavailable capabilities do not block
  other modes in automatic scheduling, while forced unavailable modes explain why.
