# IDD-0002.spec-verbgym-verb-language-training

## Intent

VerbGym is a trainer application for practising verb forms in meaningful
sentences. It acts as the second application in the shared trainer platform and
as a reference for adding future trainer applications.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0003
- IDD-0004
- IDD-0005
- IDD-0006
- IDD-0007

## Behavior

- VerbGym presents exercises from an authored catalog of everyday verb
  concepts, tense families, and sentence examples.
- A learner practises the relation between a base-language prompt and a
  learning-language answer.
- The currently available language material supports English and Spanish.
- A card is available only when its concept has the required text for both
  languages in the selected pair.
- Verb cards support pronunciation, prompt-to-answer choice, answer-to-prompt
  choice, and listening-and-choice exercises.

## Durable Architecture And Constraints

VerbGym provides its own content and branded application experience while using
the shared trainer platform described by IDD-0006 and IDD-0007.

## Non-Goals

- Replacing the shared training rules with application-specific scheduling or
  progress semantics.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A learner can practise available verb concepts in supported tense families.
- Every presented card has both a prompt and an answer for the selected language
  pair.
- VerbGym participates in the shared navigation, training, progress, and
  gamification experience.

## Verification

- Automated coverage establishes that authored verb material is valid and
  produces only cards with complete language pairs.
- Automated coverage establishes interaction-mode compatibility for verb cards.
